# University Academic Handbook

The odd semester begins in July and the even semester begins in January.

Students can register for courses through the academic portal before the published registration deadline.

The library is open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Mid-term examinations are conducted according to the examination cell timetable published on the student portal.

For academic grievances, students should first contact their department coordinator and then the dean office if the issue remains unresolved.
